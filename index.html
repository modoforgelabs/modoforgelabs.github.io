    How we build.

    Calm by default

    Fewer things on screen, gentle motion and no dark patterns. Our apps should make you feel better about your phone, not worse.

    Private on the device

    • No ads and no account
    • Your notifications, photos, notes and usage stay on your phone
    • Plain-language privacy policies

    Light on the battery

    Work happens only while you're looking. Animations pause when the screen is off, and nothing runs in the background without a reason.
